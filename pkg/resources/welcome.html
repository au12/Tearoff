Crisp Zebra labels from any app

Tearoff prints shipping labels on your Zebra (GX420d, GK420d, ZD421, ZP 450 and other ZPL printers). It finds the 4×6 label on whatever you print, including a letter-size return page from a website, a sideways label or two labels on one sheet. It snaps every barcode to the printer's dot grid.

  • Tearoff: preview, print, printer setup and status
  • Zebra printer driver: ⌘P from Safari, Chrome, Preview, Acrobat…
  • Print dialog shortcut: PDF ▾ › Print Label with Tearoff

When it's done, Tearoff opens and finds your Zebra on the network. It takes two clicks to add it as a printer.

Requires macOS 13 or later, on an Apple Silicon or Intel Mac. Click Customize on the next screen to skip the driver or the shortcut.
